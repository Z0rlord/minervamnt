Minerva Mint is an open-source, experimental Cashu ecash mint written in Rust. Users hold private bearer tokens issued by the mint; the mint's backing sits in Ark VTXOs instead of Lightning channel liquidity, and the operator can exit that backing to Bitcoin unilaterally if the Ark service fails.

The goal is freedom-tech infrastructure: ecash issuance and redemption on open protocols, with auditable liabilities and no single vendor controlling the full stack.
