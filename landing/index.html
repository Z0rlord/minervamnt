Run your own mint

Minerva Mint is open-source (AGPL-3.0), experimental Cashu infrastructure written in Rust. Anyone can operate a mint: build the service, point it at your own Bitcoin node and Ark provider, and issue ecash yourself.

How it works

Users pay a Lightning invoice and receive private bearer tokens; when they redeem, the mint pays out over Lightning. Standard Cashu protocol, custodial ecash, as with any Cashu mint.

Why Minerva

Reserves in Ark, not a Lightning wallet. A typical Cashu mint holds its reserves in Lightning channels: keys online, liquidity to manage. Minerva holds reserves in Ark VTXOs, bitcoin the operator can move back on-chain unilaterally if the Ark service fails.

Built to be accountable. A public transparency endpoint reconciles outstanding ecash against the mint's reserves; timestamped liability proofs remain under development. The aim is backing that can be checked, not just trusted.
